Healing the Warrior Spirit

This is not just a program. It's a rite of reclamation.

Guided by Indigenous teachings, movement therapy, and the Seven Sacred Teachings, Healing the Warrior Spirit is a 7-week experience for those ready to shed shame and return to strength.

Why It Exists

Colonial violence, addiction, incarceration, and silence fractured our lineages. This program helps restore what was never meant to be broken.

  • Each week explores a sacred theme: identity, discipline, grief, sovereignty, and more.
  • Includes sweat-style workouts, journal reflections, and elder-inspired rituals.
  • Designed for Indigenous men, recovery circles, and youth ready to rise.
